Why an Encyclo-Reac, an encyclopedia of reactionary speculations? One example: in the summer of 2025, the world discovered with alarm the New Gaza project, designed by BCG, an American consulting firm, in collaboration with the Israeli and American governments. This project calls for the expulsion of Gaza's residents and their replacement with brand-new office towers and high-tech factories.

The Encyclo-Reac traces the genealogy of New Gaza. Similar projects were developed as early as the 1990s in apartheid South Africa and during Somalia's collapse (see Ciskei, Freedonia). These projects shed light on what is at stake with New Gaza: a colonial, segregationist vision of territory, infused with the same supremacist ideas.

We are familiar with the reactionary speculations of the Anglo-American NRx (Dark Enlightenment, CEO-led Monarchy). Less well known are the speculative fictions fueling Indian ethno-nationalism (Akshardham), the Russian and Saudi petro-regimes, or transnational digital communities (Kek, NESARA/GESARA).

The Encyclo-Reac aims to correct this imbalance. It compiles a global inventory of the reactionary speculations shaping today's world, placing each one in context and linking it to its network of ideological and economic support. Each entry runs under 300 words, with 3 images and 3 references. Together, they shed light on the unsettling links between imagination and reactionary movements.
