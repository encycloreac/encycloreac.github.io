What is the point of Reactiopedia, the encyclopedia of reactionary speculations? One example: in July 2025, the world discovered with great concern the New Gaza project, designed by the Boston Consulting Group, a US consulting firm, in collaboration with Tony Blair's think tank and the Israeli and American governments. This project calls for the expulsion of Gaza's residents and their replacement with brand-new office towers and high-tech factories.

Reactiopedia traces the genealogy of New Gaza. Similar projects were developed a few decades ago in apartheid South Africa and during Somalia's collapse (see Ciskei, Freedonia). These projects shed light on what is at stake with New Gaza: a colonial vision of territory, infused with the same mix of racist and libertarian ideas.

We are familiar with the reactionary speculations of the Anglo-American NRx (Dark Enlightenment, CEO-led Monarchy). We know less the speculative fictions fueling Hindu ethno-nationalism (Hindu pseudohistory), the Russian and Saudi petro-regimes (Neo-eurasianism), or transnational digital communities (Kek, NESARA/GESARA).

Reactiopedia aims to correct this imbalance. It draws a global inventory of the reactionary speculations shaping today's world, placing each one in context and linking it to its network of ideological and economic support. It avoids fascination for the content of these speculations by focusing on their ideological, business, and political supporters. Each entry runs under 300 words and 3 references.
